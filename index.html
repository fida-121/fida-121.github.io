CASCADE

Ongoing research · Final year project

Collaborative Anticipatory Stacking with Collision-Aware Dynamic Execution · 2025 to present

CASCADE explores proactive human-robot collaboration in assembly and stacking tasks. The system aims to understand human actions, anticipate upcoming intentions, coordinate task allocation, monitor assembly sequences, and adapt robot motion to maintain safe collaboration.

System architecture

Input: human hands and the shared workspace (RGB-D) Output: collision-aware Panda motion (ROS 2)
  1. 01

    Perception

    RGB-D camera and hand tracking observe the person and the components.

    In progress
  2. 02

    Intention prediction

    Sequence models (LSTM, Transformer) estimate the next human action.

    Under investigation
  3. 03

    Task allocation

    The robot picks a different component from the one the person will likely take.

    In progress
  4. 04

    Sequence monitoring

    Check the assembly order, detect wrong placement and restore the sequence.

    Planned
  5. 05

    Safe execution

    Distance-based safety zones adapt the speed and path of the Panda.

    In progress
Status of each stage: In progress Under investigation Planned

Four capabilities

AIn progress

Human Intention Prediction

Use RGB-D observations to study human hand movements and predict upcoming actions: reaching, grasping, transporting, placing, retracting and idle.

LSTM and Transformer models are under investigation.

BIn progress

Anticipatory Task Allocation

The robot anticipates which component the human is likely to collect and proactively selects another suitable task, reducing competition for the same object.

CPlanned

Sequence Monitoring and Error Recovery

Monitor the required assembly sequence and detect incorrect component placement. The intended recovery is to remove or reverse the placement and restore the sequence.

DIn progress

Collision-Aware Dynamic Execution

Distance-based safety zones adapt robot behaviour, implemented in ROS 2.

Safe zoneNormal execution.
Caution zoneReduced speed.
Critical zoneTrajectory modification or stopping.

Evidence status. No dataset size, model accuracy, autonomous-recovery result or safety validation is reported yet. The planned dataset targets 60 to 100 participants with about 20 trials each.

Franka Panda Impedance Control

Completed · Semester project

Cartesian impedance control on a real Franka Emika Panda · UET Lahore · Feb to Jun 2026

A compliant controller lets the arm yield to external forces instead of tracking a path rigidly. I built a Cartesian impedance controller on libfranka that runs at 1 kHz, added kinesthetic hand-guiding with waypoint replay, and used a genetic algorithm to tune stiffness and damping.

Workflow

  1. 1. Hand-guideMove the arm by hand and record waypoints from the keyboard.
  2. 2. ReplayLinear interpolation for position, SLERP for orientation.
  3. 3. Impedance controlTorques from Cartesian pose error at 1 kHz, with gravity and Coriolis compensation.
  4. 4. TuneA genetic algorithm searches stiffness and damping.

Reported results against the libfranka default

MetricDefaultGA-optimisedChange
Final residual error7.76 mm3.10 mm−60.1%
RMS error96.57 mm71.92 mm−25.5%
Peak error220.02 mm312.81 mmhigher

The peak error is higher because the optimiser favoured steady-state convergence. The 1 mm settling threshold was not reached within the 5 s window in any configuration.

Evaluation conditions

  • Tested on a physical Panda (FCI mode, firmware 4.2.2). ROS 2 and MoveIt 2 were used for inverse kinematics only, not in the torque loop.
  • Each candidate parameter set was scored on one standardised test motion. The search used a population of 12 and up to 15 generations (up to 180 evaluations).
  • The fitness function combined RMS error, peak error, settling time, oscillation and overshoot.
  • Repeat-trial statistics and error bars are not reported yet.

Safe Robotic Motion Planning

Simulation only

MPPI + GPMP2 + Control Barrier Functions · Franka Panda · MuJoCo

A layered pipeline that plans a path to a target while avoiding a known obstacle, with a safety filter at the lowest level.

  • GPMP2 plans the global trajectory and replans from the current state.
  • MPPI is the local controller. It samples short-horizon trajectories around the reference.
  • CBF-QP filters every command with the smallest correction that keeps it safe.

Verified in simulation only. It has no real-robot interface and is not yet hardware-validated. In fast motion the barrier value can dip slightly below zero for a short time before recovering.

ML-Based Condition Monitoring

Completed

Vertical drill · 2025

Problem. The condition of a drill is hard to judge by eye. This system reads the machine's sensors and classifies its condition so maintenance can be planned.

  • Four sensors (current, vibration, temperature, humidity) sampled at 20 Hz through an Arduino.
  • Filtering, a physics-based phase detector and 35+ sliding-window features.
  • XGBoost classified 8 condition categories with a reported 98% test accuracy.
  • A Streamlit dashboard shows health score, model confidence and maintenance advice.
  • XGBoost
  • Signal filtering
  • Feature extraction
  • Streamlit
  • Arduino
