CASCADE Final Year Project

Collaborative Anticipatory Stacking with Collision-Aware Dynamic Execution · 2025 – Present

Problem. Most collaborative robots only react to what a person has already done. CASCADE explores a robot that predicts what its human partner will do next during a shared assembly task, and plans around it.

  • Building an RGB-D hand-tracking perception pipeline and a custom manipulation dataset (target: 60–100 participants, about 20 trials each) covering reaching, grasping, transporting, placing, retracting and idle.
  • Investigating LSTM and Transformer models for human intention prediction.
  • Designing anticipatory task allocation so the robot works on a complementary component, with assembly-sequence monitoring and automatic recovery from misplaced parts.
  • Implementing collision-aware execution in ROS 2 using proximity-based safe, caution and critical zones (speed reduction, trajectory modification or stopping).

Cartesian Impedance Control of the Franka Panda (7-DOF)

Semester Project · UET Lahore · Feb – Jun 2026

Problem. Safe physical interaction needs a compliant controller that tracks well without being stiff. I implemented and tuned one on real hardware.

  • Designed a real-time Cartesian impedance controller running at 1 kHz using ROS2 and libfranka, with analytical forward kinematics and Jacobian-based torque mapping.
  • Built a hand-guiding and trajectory replay module with waypoint recording and gravity/Coriolis compensation.
  • Used a genetic algorithm to jointly optimize stiffness and damping, reducing final tracking error to 3.10 mm, 60% better than the default.
  • Added a multi-tier real-time safety system (torque, error-growth and oscillation aborts) for automated hardware testing.
